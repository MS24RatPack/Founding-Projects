Customer Health Command Center

An AI-powered CS operations tool for B2B SaaS teams. Drop in your account data and get a unified dashboard that scores every customer for churn risk, tracks activation milestones, surfaces a prioritized intervention queue with ready-to-send outreach, and models the revenue impact of your expansion levers — in one command.
